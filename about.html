About me

The short version: I'm a BS Information Technology student who likes making systems — networks, software, and everything in between — actually work for the people using them.

Background

I'm a BS Information Technology (BSINT) student who got hooked on tech after fixing a family computer that "wouldn't turn on" — it turned out to be a loose cable, but the process of tracing the problem down was what stuck with me. That same instinct — start with the system, not just the symptom — is what drew me to IT over a narrower specialty: I like that one week I'm configuring a network, and the next I'm building a database-backed web app.

Through my program I've worked on class projects covering systems administration, networking, and application development, plus a couple of freelance sites for local shops and small tools I built to solve my own annoyances. I care most about the details a user or a sysadmin actually notices: does the setup doc actually work if you follow it, does the form tell you what went wrong, does the system still hold up under real conditions instead of just the demo.
